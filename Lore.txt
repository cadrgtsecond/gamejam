Far from his ancestral halls, a knight ventured into distant lands, seeking to prove his worth and earn the honours befitting his name.

Through trials and countless battles, he pursued the glory that would complete his banner, and the day he might return before his sovereign.

Yet, whilst he wandered, his homeland fell to ruin. Its walls were broken, its banners silenced, and its people scattered to the winds.

Unbeknownst to him, the kingdom he had sworn to serve was no longer as he had left it.

And at last, with banner in hand, the knight returned home.





//////////////////////////////////////////////////



The throne stands empty.

The kingdom lies in ashes.

Those who once stood in its defense are gone.

And before the throne of his fallen king, the knight kneels.

His banner bears no glory. Only remembrance.
